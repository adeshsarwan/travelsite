Come for the waterfront walk. Stay for the pier, skyline and the slower side of South Beach.

WHY GOWANDRIA PICKS IT

South Beach,
without the rush.

At the southern tip of Miami Beach, South Pointe Park gives you green space, direct beach access, a waterfront promenade and the 450-foot South Pointe Pier — with views toward Fisher Island, PortMiami, Downtown Miami and the South Beach shoreline.

It works especially well as the beginning or end of a South Beach day because you can continue directly onto the Miami Beach Beachwalk rather than treating the park as an isolated attraction.

Verified against City of Miami Beach and Greater Miami & Miami Beach visitor information · September 2026.

HOW WE'D DO IT

Give it
90 minutes.

01

Start on the promenade

Walk the waterfront edge and let the views toward Fisher Island and the shipping channel set the pace.

02

Walk out on the pier

The 450-foot pier is the obvious viewpoint. Watch the water, anglers and boats rather than rushing back.

03

Choose park or beach

Families can use the playground and splash area; beach travelers can continue directly onto the sand.

04

Continue north

Instead of turning around, follow the Beachwalk north toward the Art Deco side of South Beach.
